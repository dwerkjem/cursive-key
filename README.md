# Cursive Key

A free website that teaches people, especially genealogists, to read historical cursive handwriting using **real documents** rather than modern copybook samples.

## Why

Census pages, deeds, wills, and letters from the 1700s–1900s are hard to read. The existing free resources (BYU Script Tutorial, UK National Archives palaeography tutorial) are excellent but focus mainly on 1500–1800 British hands. Nothing free drills learners on large numbers of real, short snippets of **18th–20th century American cursive** with an answer key and spaced repetition.

## Core ideas

- **Real snippets:** words and lines cropped from public-domain manuscripts, each with a verified transcription and a link to its source.
- **Practice that adapts:** type what you see, check your answer, and let spaced repetition bring back the snippets you missed.
- **Letterform galleries:** many real examples of each letter, abbreviation, and common record term.
- **Free forever on no budget:** a static site on Cloudflare with progress stored in the learner's browser. An optional sync can be added within free-tier limits.
