# Cursive Key

A free site that teaches people, especially genealogists, to read historical cursive from real document snippets.

## Language

### Content

**Snippet**:
A small image of one word or line cropped from a real historical document, with its verified transcription and source.
_Avoid_: Card, sample, clip, example

### People

**Person**:
Anyone known to the site. A Person holds one or more Roles at the same time.
_Avoid_: User, account, member

**Role**:
A named set of things a Person may do on the site. Roles combine; they don't replace one another.
_Avoid_: Permission level, user type

**Learner**:
The Role of practising reading Snippets.
_Avoid_: Student, user

**Beta Tester**:
A Learner who has been granted access to the site during the Closed Beta.
_Avoid_: Early adopter, tester

**Curator**:
The Role of cropping and transcribing Snippets and publishing them.
_Avoid_: Editor, contributor, transcriber

**Admin**:
The Role of running the site itself, as distinct from its content.
_Avoid_: Moderator, superuser, owner

### Launch

**Closed Beta**:
The period when only the Landing Page and Access Request form are public, and everything else is limited to Beta Testers.
_Avoid_: Private beta, early access, alpha

**Public Launch**:
The moment the site opens to everyone and the Closed Beta ends. After it, signing in is optional and only needed for Role-specific work and Personal Settings.
_Avoid_: GA, release, go-live

**Personal Settings**:
Preferences that belong to one signed-in Person rather than to a browser.
_Avoid_: User settings, profile, preferences

**Landing Page**:
The public page that explains the site during the Closed Beta and links to the Access Request form.
_Avoid_: Splash page, home page

**Access Request**:
A Person's request to become a Beta Tester.
_Avoid_: Signup, application, waitlist entry

### Feedback

**Feedback**:
Something a visitor tells the site's maintainers on purpose: a Bug Report, a Transcription Report, or a Suggestion. It stays private until an Admin Promotes it.
_Avoid_: Ticket, issue, comment

**Bug Report**:
Feedback that something on the site is broken.
_Avoid_: Bug ticket, defect

**Transcription Report**:
Feedback that a specific Snippet's transcription is wrong.
_Avoid_: Correction, erratum, wrong-answer report

**Suggestion**:
Feedback proposing something new or different.
_Avoid_: Feature request, idea

**Error Report**:
A failure the site captures by itself, with no one filing it.
_Avoid_: Crash, exception, log

**Promote**:
An Admin turning Feedback or an Error Report into a public issue for triage, with personal details removed.
_Avoid_: Escalate, forward, publish
